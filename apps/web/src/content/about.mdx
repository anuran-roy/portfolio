## What I've done so far

> The journey IS the destination

Yep, that's what I follow. I've been a journeyman throughout my learning arc - never been someone to concentrate on doing one thing for life.

I contributed to [AI4Bharat's Shoonya](https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/) at IIT Madras, an annotation platform spanning 22 languages that powered India's Bhashini initiative. I worked at Google DeepMind and at CloudSEK, where I applied AI to cybersecurity. I built EdgeChains at Arakoo, a framework later absorbed into [Spring AI](https://spring.io/projects/spring-ai). I completed an AI Safety fellowship by Impact Academy, focused on Mechanistic Interpretability.

Each of these taught me something specific. AI4Bharat showed me what building for Indian languages looks like at national scale. DeepMind gave me depth at the frontier. CloudSEK put AI in production against adversarial data. EdgeChains taught me that developer tooling is where leverage lives. All of it feeds into what I'm building now.
