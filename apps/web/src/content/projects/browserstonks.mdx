BrowserStonks is a Chrome extension to extend the capabilities of your desktop harness. It lets you unify your entire computer experience into one seamless way, not separated into two distinct worlds: the browser and the desktop.

Pair it with the harness of your choice - Pi, Hermes or OpenCode. You fundamentally get native web browser capabilities with browsing, such that your browser naturally becomes a part of your AI harness that can be used by different models.
