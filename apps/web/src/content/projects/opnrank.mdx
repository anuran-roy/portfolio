**OpnRank** is a lightweight, extensible SEO metrics scanner for analyzing
search-engine-optimization data. Built with Seaborn, TextBlob, Text2Emotion,
Pandas, and Pytrends.

> OpnRank has since been merged into [OpnEco](/projects/opneco).
